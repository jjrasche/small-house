Heat per foot of greenhouse

Per foot of length, at a 30 F difference unless stated. engine = the model worked it out. hand = my own arithmetic.

Seventy loses about 720 W per foot at a 30 F difference engine, conditional (50,448 W over the 70 ft house, 0.83 W per ft³ of air). At 50 F it is 1,201 W per foot (1.39 W per ft³). Your 2 W per ft³ is more than double the 30 F figure. Of the 30 F total: walls and glass 37,092 W, air leaks 9,570 W, ground 3,786 W.

My hand count agrees within 4%. Glass 352 and 25 W, north wall 8 W, end walls 122 W, air leaks 137 W: 643 W per foot, plus the model's own ground 54 W gives 697 hand. The model's walls-and-glass figure is 530 W per foot against my 506. The ground term is the model's alone; I cannot derive it by hand. Both of the model's heat calculations now give the same answer, which they did not before, after domains fixed the air layers and added the end walls. Conditional on 11 stated assumptions.

What it would take at 30 F: about 14,100 ft³ of aerated insulated compost box (12,400 to 16,500), or 706 kWh of night store.

Sun: December averages 1,218 Wh/m² a day on flat ground, 22% less than January. The glass slopes only 12 degrees, so it catches 1.1 to 1.45 times a flat surface. The 480 W figure was a January number. The model has no hour-by-hour sun on a sloped surface yet.

Compost: the model's one measured trench gives 1.6 W/ft³ (6.5 when hot, 149 F), but only for a small 2 ft trench. A 45 ft wide, 5 to 10 ft deep mass has not been run. Open piles in the research run 0.5 to 2.7 W/ft³; a closed aerated box 3 to 4.

5. May in December (55 to 75 F, hour by hour)

Not run yet. The one-foot slice now exists; it still needs: sun on a sloped surface, quad-wall heat loss (no source), and the 24 winters of hourly weather (banked and checked). Worst stretch in them: 14 days under half of full sun, starting December 14, 2022. Coldest three days: −17.7 C, January 30 to February 1, 2019. Sun there is a satellite estimate, so results stay conditional.

One catch: a 30 F difference in the coldest average week is air at 54 F. Tilapia stop eating below 61 F and die near 55 F. Fish water needs 71.6 F, about a 48 F difference that week.

Rough water needed for dark days with zero sun hand: 29 to 53 ft³ per foot for 3 days, 49 to 88 for 5. The real record says long stretches are half-sun, not no-sun.
