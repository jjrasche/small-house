## Words

- **Charter:** the brief for one delegated task: goal, inputs, what it may touch, acceptance commands, deadline, budget. It ends when its acceptance passes.
- **Job:** a running thing: a gate run, a build ticket, a cron, a background agent. bgstatus lists it, and it stops at its deadline. A charter may start jobs; a job is never the brief.
- **Gate:** the worlds' expected figures, which every engine or domain merge must pass.
- **Ledger:** one tracked file of promises with dates, on the working branch.
